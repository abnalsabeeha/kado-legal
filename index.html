KADO — Terms & Privacy

Last updated August 2026

The short version

KADO builds meal plans from real UAE restaurant menus, tuned to your goals. To do that we save what you tell us and how you use the app. We never sell your data. You can delete it any time. The details are below.

What we collect

  • Your profile: name, gender, age, height, weight, activity level, goal, dietary preferences and allergens, and the area you order in.
  • Your activity: the plans we generate, meals you mark as eaten, weights you log, dishes you swap or rate, and basic in-app events (e.g. screens opened), so we can improve your recommendations and the product.
  • Device: a device identifier and an anonymous account id so your data stays with you without requiring an email or password.

Health & fitness data (Apple Health, WHOOP)

You can optionally connect KADO to Apple Health or to your WHOOP account to show calories burned next to what you eat.

  • Apple Health: KADO reads your Active and Resting Energy (calories burned) with your permission, on your device only. KADO never writes to Apple Health, and your Health data is not uploaded to our servers.
  • WHOOP: if you connect WHOOP, your sign-in happens on whoop.com and your WHOOP data (e.g. daily energy expenditure) travels directly between your device and WHOOP. We do not store your WHOOP data or your WHOOP password on our servers; access tokens are kept securely on your device.
  • You can disconnect either source at any time in Profile → Connected apps. Disconnecting stops all reading immediately. Health data is used only to show your burn and balance your plan — never for advertising, and never sold.

How we use your data

We use your profile and activity to build and adapt your meal plans, show your progress, and improve KADO. This is the personalisation you consent to when you start: KADO learns from your preferences, ratings, and choices to tailor the plan to you. We do not sell your personal data or share it for advertising.

Estimated nutrition (important)

Restaurants don't always publish full nutrition, so some calorie and macro values in KADO are estimated rather than measured. Estimated values are clearly marked. KADO is a planning tool, not medical or dietary advice — talk to a qualified professional for medical decisions.

Who KADO is for

KADO offers calorie and weight-management planning and is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not designed for use by minors.

Your rights (UAE PDPL)

You can view, correct, export, or delete your data at any time from the app, or by contacting us. Deleting your profile removes your personal data from our systems. We keep data only as long as needed to provide the service.
