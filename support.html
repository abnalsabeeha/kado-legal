Common questions

How do I delete my account? Profile → More → Delete account. Everything tied to your account is erased immediately.

I signed in as a guest — can I keep my data? Yes. Profile → Account → Sign in with Apple or email links your account without losing anything.

A dish’s nutrition looks wrong. KADO only shows restaurant-published macros for delivery dishes; if a restaurant changes its menu the next refresh picks it up. Tell us the dish and we’ll check it.
