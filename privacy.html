KADO — Terms & Privacy

Last updated September 2026 · KADO Lifestyle Solutions, Abu Dhabi, UAE

The short version

KADO builds meal plans from real UAE restaurant menus, tuned to your goals. To do that we save what you tell us and how you use the app. We never sell your data. You can delete it any time. The details are below.

What we collect

  • Your profile: name, gender, age, height, weight, activity level, goal, dietary preferences and allergens, and the area you order in.
  • Your activity: the plans we generate, meals you mark as eaten (including which restaurant and delivery platform they came from and whether you swapped them), weights and water you log, and basic in-app events (e.g. screens opened), so we can improve your recommendations and the product.
  • Your account: if you sign in with Apple or email we store the email address (Apple lets you hide it behind a relay) and, for Apple, the name you choose to share. Guests get an anonymous account id instead — no email or password.
  • A device identifier so your data stays with you across app updates.
  • Optional health data: if you connect Apple Health or WHOOP, calories burned and workouts, used only to adjust your daily calorie target. We never write to Apple Health without asking.
  • Optional location: when you tap “Use my location”, a one-off position to pick the nearest delivery area. We do not track you in the background.

How we use it

We use your profile and activity to build and adapt your meal plans, show your progress, and improve KADO. This is the personalisation you consent to when you start: KADO learns from your preferences, ratings, and choices to tailor the plan to you. We do not sell your personal data or share it for advertising, and we do not use it to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Where it lives

Your data is stored with Supabase (hosted on Amazon Web Services, Mumbai region) under an account that only you and KADO can access. Restaurant menu data is public information from Talabat and Deliveroo.

Estimated nutrition (important)

Restaurants don’t always publish full nutrition, so some calorie and macro values in KADO are estimated rather than measured. Estimated values are clearly marked. KADO is a planning tool, not medical or dietary advice — talk to a qualified professional for medical decisions.

Who KADO is for

KADO offers calorie and weight-management planning and is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not designed for use by minors.

Your rights (UAE PDPL)

You can view, correct, export, or delete your data at any time from the app (Profile → More → Delete account), or by contacting us. Deleting your account removes your personal data from our systems. We keep data only as long as needed to provide the service.

Terms of use

  • KADO is provided as-is for personal, non-commercial use. Meal availability and prices are set by the restaurants and delivery platforms, not KADO.
  • Orders are placed with Talabat or Deliveroo under their terms; KADO only links you there.
  • Don’t misuse the service (scraping, reverse engineering, or interfering with other users).
  • We may update these terms; material changes are announced in the app and require your acceptance again.
